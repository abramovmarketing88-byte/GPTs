# Example Market Research Report

## Scenario

```yaml
product_idea: "AI assistant that helps small online schools understand why students do not complete courses and recommends interventions"
industry: "EdTech SaaS"
geography: "US/EU"
business_model: "B2B subscription"
current_stage: "idea"
```

## 1. Executive Summary

- Recommended focus: cohort-based course creators and small online schools with paid programs and visible completion/revenue pressure.
- Priority job: understand where and why learners disengage so the team can improve completion without manually analyzing fragmented data.
- Highest risks: problem severity, willingness-to-pay, data access, and whether recommendations are trusted.
- Evidence status: hypothesis-only example; requires interviews, analytics access review, competitor/source research, and smoke test.

## 2. Product Idea Interpretation

| Claim | Type | Evidence | Confidence | Validation Needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Course teams struggle to diagnose drop-off reasons | Hypothesis | Plausible workflow pain, not validated here | Low | 12–15 problem interviews |
| Small schools may pay for actionable retention insights | Hypothesis | Existing analytics tools imply budget, not segment-specific proof | Low | Pricing conversations, fake-door test |

## 3. Market Map

- Direct alternatives: learning analytics tools, LMS analytics modules.
- Indirect alternatives: spreadsheets, manual student surveys, consultants, community managers.
- Non-consumption: teams ignore drop-off or make content changes based on intuition.
- Channels: creator communities, LMS app marketplaces, EdTech newsletters, founder-led outreach.

## 8. Segment Prioritization

Cohort-based creators and small online schools are recommended for first validation because pain is likely tied to revenue, channels are more accessible than enterprise/university procurement, and MVP can be tested manually.

## 9. Recommended Focus

1. Cohort-based course creators.
2. Small online schools with paid programs.

First validation:

- 10–12 problem interviews;
- review anonymized analytics workflows;
- landing page smoke test;
- concierge diagnostic report for 3 teams.

## 10. Key Risks

| Risk | Type | Why Critical | Validation Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teams do not see completion as urgent | Problem risk | No urgency means weak adoption | Problem interviews |
| Data access is too hard | Technical/operational risk | Product cannot produce insight | Integration discovery |
| Recommendations are not trusted | Value risk | Users may ignore output | Concierge MVP |

## 11. Research Plan

| Step | Method | Success Criteria | Decision Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem interviews | 7/10 mention current painful manual diagnosis | Continue if pain is repeated and recent |
| 2 | Concierge reports | 2/3 teams act on recommendations | Continue to prototype |
| 3 | Pricing test | 3+ serious budget signals | Refine pricing or segment |

## 12. Open Questions

- Which LMS integrations matter first?
- Who is the buyer: founder, program manager, or ops lead?
- What minimum insight is trusted enough to trigger action?
