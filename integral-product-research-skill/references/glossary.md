# Glossary

## AJTBD

Advanced Jobs To Be Done. A methodology for understanding what progress a person or organization seeks in a specific situation, including functional, emotional, social, contextual, and outcome dimensions.

## Job

The progress a user wants to make in a specific context. A job is not a feature, product category, or demographic label.

## Main job

The central job the product must help accomplish.

## Related job

A connected job that happens before, after, or alongside the main job.

## Functional job

A practical task the user needs to complete.

## Emotional job

A feeling the user wants to achieve or avoid.

## Social job

How the user wants to be perceived by others.

## Desired outcome

A measurable improvement the user wants, such as less time, lower risk, better accuracy, lower cost, greater confidence, or easier collaboration.

## Struggling moment

A moment of friction, anxiety, delay, risk, cost, or confusion that creates demand for a better solution.

## Current alternative

The solution, workaround, service, spreadsheet, manual process, or non-consumption pattern used today.

## Hiring criteria

Reasons a user chooses a solution.

## Firing criteria

Reasons a user abandons a current alternative.

## ABCDX

A segment classification method: A, B, C, D, or X depending on attractiveness, evidence, and constraints.

## RAT

Riskiest Assumption Test. A method for finding and testing the assumptions most likely to kill the product.

## Problem risk

Risk that the problem does not exist or is not painful enough.

## Segment risk

Risk that the selected segment is not the right segment.

## Value risk

Risk that the proposed value is not important enough.

## Willingness-to-pay risk

Risk that users or buyers are not willing to pay.

## Channel risk

Risk that the segment cannot be reached efficiently.

## Solution risk

Risk that the solution does not help complete the job.

## Usability risk

Risk that users cannot understand or use the solution.

## Operational risk

Risk that the product/service cannot be delivered sustainably.

## Legal/compliance risk

Risk caused by laws, regulations, policies, permissions, or industry compliance requirements.

## Technical risk

Risk that implementation is too complex, expensive, unreliable, or unstable.

## TAM

Total Addressable Market: total theoretical market for the job or category.

## SAM

Serviceable Available Market: part of TAM reachable within geography, model, platform, and constraints.

## SOM

Serviceable Obtainable Market: realistic initial share the team can capture.

## MVP

Minimum viable product version that validates the key job and main value hypothesis with minimal scope.
